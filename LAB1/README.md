# SE Lab 1 — Warehouse Inventory & Pallet Location Tracker

**Problem Statement #27** | Smart Cities, Transport & Logistics
**Course:** PES University — Dept. of CSE — Lab 1: Requirements Engineering & UML Use-Case Modelling

## Overview
An automated warehouse management system tracking 3D shelf pallet positions, enforcing shelf
maximum weight limits, and logging stock movements via barcode/RFID scanning.

**Actors:** Warehouse Operator, Logistics Supervisor
